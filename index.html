Welcome to Siegen Global Manufacturing Pvt Ltd

Siegen Global Manufacturing Pvt Ltd is a privately owned and professionally managed tooling enterprise founded by NTTF alumni with over 16 years of hands-on experience in the design, manufacturing, and supply of Injection Moulds, Sheet Metal Dies, Die Casting Dies, and Jigs & Fixtures across India and global markets.

With state-of-the-art tool room infrastructure, cutting-edge CNC machining centers, and a dedicated team of design engineers, we deliver reliable, high-precision tooling solutions that meet exact customer tolerances, stringent quality norms, and competitive project timelines.
